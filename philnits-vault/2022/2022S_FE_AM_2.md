# 2022S_FE_AM_2
For non-negative integer A, which of the following has the same value as (A mod 32) + 64?
Here, mod, +, AND, and OR are remainder-after-division, arithmetic addition, bitwise-
AND, and bitwise-OR operators, respectively.

a) (A AND 31) OR 64
b) (A AND 32) OR 32
c) (A OR 31) AND 64
d) (A OR 64) AND 32
?
a) (A AND 31) OR 64
