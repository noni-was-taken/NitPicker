# 2025S_FE-A_60
Company A purchased a machine for an original cost of $126,000. The salvage value
after 6 years is $6,000. What is the annual depreciation expense (in dollars) by using the
straight-line method?

a) 12,000
b) 20,000
c) 21,000
d) 36,000
?
b) 20,000
