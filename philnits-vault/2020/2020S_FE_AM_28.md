# 2020S_FE_AM_28
A sequence of two relational algebra expressions is shown below.

$T_1 \leftarrow \pi_Y(R)$

$T \leftarrow T_1 - \pi_Y((S \times T_1) - R)$

Here, “$\pi_Y$”, “$\times$”, and “$-$” represent projection, direct product, and difference, respectively. When the relational states of R and S are as follows, which of the following can be obtained as T?

[2020S_28_fig.png]

a) T(Y) = {NULL}
b) T(Y) = {A}
c) T(Y) = {B}
d) T(Y) = {A, B}
?
b) T(Y) = {A}
