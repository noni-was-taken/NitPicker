# 2019S_FE_AM_3

4, 3, 5, and 6, respectively? Here, symbols ‘’, ‘’, ‘’, and ‘’ represent the arithmetic
operators of addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, respectively.
A 	B 	2 	 	 	D 	B 	 	C 	 	

a) -3
b) 0
c) 3
d) 40/3
?
b) 0
