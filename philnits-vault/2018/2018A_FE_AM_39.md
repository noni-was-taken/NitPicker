# 2018A_FE_AM_39
An administrator captures network packets and discovers that hundreds of ICMP packets
have been sent to the host. However, it is not a particularly busy time of the day. Which
of the following is the most likely the attack executed against the computer in this
situation?

a) Denial of service
b) Man-in-the-middle
c) Spoofing
d) Worm
?
a) Denial of service
