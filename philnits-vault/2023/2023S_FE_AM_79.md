# 2023S_FE_AM_79
A company plans to provide a product with a fixed cost of $128,000, a variable cost per
unit of $48, and a selling price per unit of $80. Which of the following is the point of sales (in
dollars) to become break-even?

a) 240,000
b) 320,000
c) 480,000
d) 560,000
?
b) 320,000
