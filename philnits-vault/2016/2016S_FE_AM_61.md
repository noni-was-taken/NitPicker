# 2016S_FE_AM_61
In the Information Technology Infrastructure Library (ITIL), lowering an indicator that
represents maintainability is given as an example of a key performance indicator (KPI) for
availability management. Which of the following corresponds to this indicator?

a) Mean time between failures
b) Mean time between service incidents
c) Mean time to restore services
d) The number of interruptions within a fixed period
?
c) Mean time to restore services
