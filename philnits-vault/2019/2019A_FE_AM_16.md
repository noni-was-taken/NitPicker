# 2019A_FE_AM_16
There exists a device with an MTBF of forty-five (45) hours and an MTTR of five (5)
hours. What is the availability of a system that connects two (2) such devices in series?

a) 0.81
b) 0.90
c) 0.95
d) 0.99
?
a) 0.81
