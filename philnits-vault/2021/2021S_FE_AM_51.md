# 2021S_FE_AM_51
Based on the definition of project management process groups in ISO 21500, which of
the following is an activity that is to be implemented in the planning process group among
the project management processes?

a) To define scope
b) To develop a project charter
c) To identify stakeholders
d) To perform quality assurance
?
a) To define scope
