# 2024S_FE-A_19
Which of the following is the appropriate interpretation of the E-R model shown below?

a) A department has many employees, and employees can have multiple dependents.
b) A department has many employees, where each employee has exactly one dependent.
c) An employee can have at most one dependent.
d) An employee can work in two departments.
?
a) A department has many employees, and employees can have multiple dependents.
