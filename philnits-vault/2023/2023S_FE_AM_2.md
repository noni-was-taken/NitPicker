# 2023S_FE_AM_2
When the hexadecimal value ABCD in a 32-bit register is logically shifted right by two bits,
which of the following is the resulting value in hexadecimal?

a) 2AF3
b) 6AF3
c) AF34
d) EAF3
?
a) 2AF3
