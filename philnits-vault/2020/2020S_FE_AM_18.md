# 2020S_FE_AM_18
Assume that there are three pages in the real memory that can be allocated by the LRU
method. Which of the following is the number of page faults for the page reference string
shown below? Here, three pages are initially empty, and filling of an empty page is not
considered as a page replacement operation. The numbers are the page IDs that come to the
system from left to right.
Page reference string 	2 	4 	1 	1 	2 	7 	3 	4 	6 	5 	5 	1 	2

[2020S_18_fig.png]

a) 4
b) 5
c) 6
d) 7
?
d) 7
