# 2016S_FE_AM_80
Which of the following is a criminal act related to a computer virus according to the
Convention on Cybercrime?

a) A person creates a new virus to develop and test antivirus software.
b) A person forwards a received e-mail that is infected with a virus without being aware of the infection.
c) A person who found a virus that was created by a stranger stores the virus for the purpose that he/she can later run it on another person’s computer.
d) A person who received and found a virus offers it to an antivirus organization with a clear indication of a virus’s presence.
?
c) A person who found a virus that was created by a stranger stores the virus for the purpose that he/she can later run it on another person’s computer.
