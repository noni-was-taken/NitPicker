# 2026S_FE-A_21
There is a relation schema A = (P, Q, R, S, T, Y) with functional dependencies below:
• 	P → Q
• 	QR → S
• 	T → R
• 	S → P
Which of the following is the candidate keys of A?

a) PT, QT
b) PT, QT, ST
c) PTY, QTY, QRY
d) PTY, QTY, STY
?
d) PTY, QTY, STY
