# 2018S_FE_AM_33
Which of the following is a pair of IP addresses belonging to the same subnet, when the
subnet mask is 255.255.255.240?

a) 192.168.1.14 and 192.168.1.17
b) 192.168.1.17 and 192.168.1.29
c) 192.168.1.29 and 192.168.1.33
d) 192.168.1.33 and 192.168.1.49
?
b) 192.168.1.17 and 192.168.1.29
