# 2020S_FE_AM_62
When a workflow system is used for business improvement, which of the following is an
appropriate effect that can be expected?

a) A standard protocol used for data exchange between a company and its customers can be provided.
b) Processing speed for office procedures from document submission to approval can be improved.
c) Services such as discounts can be provided according to the purchase amount of a customer.
d) Shipping and receiving of stocked products can be automated in a warehouse.
?
b) Processing speed for office procedures from document submission to approval can be improved.
