Part 1: Service management system requirements), which of the following is an appropriate
description of problem management?

b) It is a process used to identify the root cause of an incident, minimize the adverse impact of the incident, and prevent it from occurring again. [2016S_59_opt_b.png]
c) It is a process used to provide a single point of contact between IT service users and the service provider. [2016S_59_opt_c.png]
d) It is a process used to restore normal service operations as soon as possible to minimize the adverse impact of an incident. [2016S_59_opt_d.png]
