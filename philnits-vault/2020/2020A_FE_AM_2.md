# 2020A_FE_AM_2
A contradiction is a Boolean expression that always evaluates as FALSE for any assignment of truth values to its variables. Which of the following is a contradiction? Here, "$\cdot$", "$+$", and "$\overline{\phantom{x}}$" represent the AND, OR, and NOT operators, respectively.

a) $(p \cdot (\overline{p} + q)) \cdot \overline{q}$
b) $(p \cdot \overline{q}) \cdot (\overline{p} + \overline{q})$
c) $p + (p \cdot q)$
d) $\overline{p} + (p \cdot \overline{q}) + q$
?
a) $(p \cdot (\overline{p} + q)) \cdot \overline{q}$
