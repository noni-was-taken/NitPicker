# 2019A_FE_AM_71
Which of the following is an explanation of knowledge management?

a) A flat organization structure with minimal hierarchical layers is maintained to accelerate decision-making.
b) Comparative analysis with other successful companies is performed to drive management innovation.
c) Knowledge scattered across an enterprise is shared to improve overall problem-solving capability.
d) Management is centered on proprietary know-how and technology that are not easy for other companies to imitate.
?
c) Knowledge scattered across an enterprise is shared to improve overall problem-solving capability.
