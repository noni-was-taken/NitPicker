# 2026S_FE-A_7
For integers x and y (x > y ≥ 0), a function F(x, y) is defined as below:
F(x, y) = x (when y = 0); F(x, y) = F(y, x mod y) (when y > 0).
What is the value F(231, 15)? Here, “x mod y” represents the remainder after division of x by y.

[2026S_7_fig.png]

a) 2
b) 3
c) 5
d) 7
?
b) 3
