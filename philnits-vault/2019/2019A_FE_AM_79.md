# 2019A_FE_AM_79
Which of the following inventory valuation methods calculates the inventory unit price
by dividing the total purchase amount by the total quantity of items purchased at the end of
the accounting period?

a) First-in- first-out method
b) Last-in- first-out method
c) Moving average method
d) Periodic average method
?
d) Periodic average method
