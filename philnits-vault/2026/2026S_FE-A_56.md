# 2026S_FE-A_56
Which of the following is part of total quality management?

a) Designing products and services that meet or exceed customers’ expectations
b) Focusing on the appointment of staff with long-term work experience in a similar environment
c) Prioritizing central decisions rather than empowerment to ensure the quality of products and services
d) Promoting the capability of each department to work independently in a competitive manner
?
a) Designing products and services that meet or exceed customers’ expectations
