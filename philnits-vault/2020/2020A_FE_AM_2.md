# 2020A_FE_AM_2
A contradiction is a Boolean expression that always evaluates as FALSE for any assignment
of truth values to its variables. Which of the following is a contradiction? Here, " ⋅ ", " ൅ ",
and " ഥ " represent the AND, OR, and NOT operators, respectively.

a) ൫ ݌⋅ ሺ݌ഥ ൅ ݍሻ൯ ⋅ ݍത
b) ݍ ⋅ ݌ሺതሻ ݌ሺ ⋅ഥ ൅ ݍതሻ
c) ݌൅ ሺ ݌⋅ ݍሻ
d) ݌ഥ ൅ ሺ ݌⋅ ݍതሻ ൅ ݍ
?
a) ൫ ݌⋅ ሺ݌ഥ ൅ ݍሻ൯ ⋅ ݍത
