# 2021A_FE_AM_61
Which of the following is an appropriate feasibility evaluation when checking whether
the lifetime benefits of the proposed information system is greater than its lifetime costs?

a) Economic feasibility
b) Operational feasibility
c) Scheduling feasibility
d) Technical feasibility
?
