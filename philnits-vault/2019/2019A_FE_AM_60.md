# 2019A_FE_AM_60
Among the implementation structures of a system audit, which of the following is the
most important to avoid from the standpoint of the independence of a system auditor?

a) Mr. A of the general affairs department, who was appointed as a member of the audit team, audits the status of entrance and exit control for the general affairs department together with other team members.
b) Mr. B of the audit department audits the status of personal information management by an external contractor to whom his company outsources business operations that deal with personal information.
c) Mr. C, who was transferred to the audit department five (5) years ago from a development manager position in the information system department, audits the status of Internet use in the marketing department.
d) Mr. D of the legal department cooperates with an auditor in an audit of the validity of an outsourcing contract in response to a request from the audit department.
?
a) Mr. A of the general affairs department, who was appointed as a member of the audit team, audits the status of entrance and exit control for the general affairs department together with other team members.
