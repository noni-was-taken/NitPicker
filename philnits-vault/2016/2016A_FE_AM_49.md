# 2016A_FE_AM_49
Which of the following is an appropriate category of system maintenance preformed for
the purpose of modifying the system to cope with changes in the software environment (e.g.
DBMS, Operating system)?

a) Adaptive maintenance [2016A_49_opt_a.png]
b) Corrective maintenance [2016A_49_opt_b.png]
c) Perfective maintenance [2016A_49_opt_c.png]
d) Preventive maintenance [2016A_49_opt_d.png]
?
a) Adaptive maintenance
