EMP, DEPT, and DEPT_LOCS are created as shown below for recording the employee,
department, and department location data, respectively.
EMP 	DEPT 	DEPT_LOCS

SELECT EName, Salary
FROM EMP
WHERE DNO IN (( SELECT DNO
FROM 	DEPT)
MINUS
(SELECT DNO
FROM DEPT_LOCS
WHERE Region=’L2’)
