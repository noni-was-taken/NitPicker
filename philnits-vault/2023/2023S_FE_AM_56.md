# 2023S_FE_AM_56
When an IT service is discontinued, it is important to comprehensively identify the assets
that were used and appropriately remove or free surplus assets. Which of the following is an
event that may occur due to mistaken removal of assets that should remain, but does not
occur because of the removal of assets that should be removed?

a) Occurrence of incidents involving other IT services that share assets with the discontinued IT service
b) Overpaying software or hardware maintenance charges
c) Wasting money on software licenses
d) Wasting space that becomes unused on magnetic disks
?
a) Occurrence of incidents involving other IT services that share assets with the discontinued IT service
