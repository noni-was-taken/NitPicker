# 2016A_FE_AM_41
Biometric authentication includes a method of authentication by extracting physical
characteristics, and a method of authentication by extracting behavior characteristics. Which
of the following has behavior characteristics?

a) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics from the branching angle of the branching point of a blood vessel, and the length between the branching points [2016A_41_opt_a.png]
b) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics from the speed of and pressure on a pen when a signature is written [2016A_41_opt_b.png]
c) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics of chaotic wrinkles spreading out from the pupil [2016A_41_opt_c.png]
d) Performing authentication by extracting the characteristic points called minutiae from the patterns formed by ridge lines [2016A_41_opt_d.png]
?
b) Performing authentication by extracting characteristics from the speed of and pressure on a pen when a signature is written [2016A_41_opt_b.png]
