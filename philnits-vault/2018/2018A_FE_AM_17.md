given in milliseconds, and the processes arrive in the order of P1, P2, P3, P4, and P5
with the arrival time of all processes at t = 0.
Process 	Burst Time
P1 	14
P2 	5
P3 	6
P4 	5
P5 	7
Which of the following is the average turnaround time of five (5) processes from P1
through P5 with the first-come first-served (FCFS) scheduling algorithm?

a) 10 [2018A_17_opt_a.png]
b) 15
c) 20 [2018A_17_opt_c.png]
d) 25 [2018A_17_opt_d.png]
