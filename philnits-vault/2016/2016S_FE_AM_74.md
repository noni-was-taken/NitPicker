# 2016S_FE_AM_74
Which of the following is an explanation of a smart grid?

a) It is a system that aims at the utilization of recyclable energy, supply of stable electric power, and optimum demand adjustment through the integrated control of power generation and power consumption based on communication and information-processing technology. [2016S_74_opt_a.png]
b) It is a system that can provide ideal health advice to each individual by performing analysis based on similar case reports, such as the result of health examinations and medication use. [2016S_74_opt_b.png]
c) It is a system that can search for information on restaurants and entertainment, send and receive traffic information, and communicate the current position at the time of an emergency by using information-processing equipment installed in vehicles. [2016S_74_opt_c.png]
d) It is a system that supports varied work styles by enabling mutual communication between workers with varied working styles, such as home-based employees and senior workers. [2016S_74_opt_d.png]
?
a) It is a system that aims at the utilization of recyclable energy, supply of stable electric power, and optimum demand adjustment through the integrated control of power generation and power consumption based on communication and information-processing technology.
