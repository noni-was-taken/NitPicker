# 2023A_FE_AM_5
Which of the following expressions in postfix notation is equivalent to the expression
2+5×3+4 in infix notation? Here, the operator “×” has higher precedence than “+”.

a) 2 5 3 4 × + +
b) 2 5 + 3 4 × +
c) 2 5 3 + × 4 +
d) 2 5 3 × + 4 +
?
d) 2 5 3 × + 4 +
