management?

a) A capacity plan is created, implemented, and maintained while human, technical, informational, and financial resources are taken into consideration.
b) A service catalog and SLA are created for the service to be provided, and they are agreed upon with the customer.
c) Costs are monitored and reported against the budget; the financial forecasts are reviewed, and costs are managed.
d) The risks to service continuity and availability of services are assessed and documented.
