# 2020A_FE_AM_66
According to the “competitive strategy” proposed by Philip Kotler, which of the
following describes a company that has no high market share but utilizes quantitatively large
management resources?

a) Market challenger
b) Market follower
c) Market leader
d) Market nicher
?
a) Market challenger
