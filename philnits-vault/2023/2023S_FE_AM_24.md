# 2023S_FE_AM_24
What is the approximate minimum required bandwidth for delivering a 30-frames/second
video in 800 x 600 pixel and 24-bit full color? Here, the data is not compressed during
communication.

a) 350 kbps
b) 3.5 Mbps
c) 35 Mbps
d) 350 Mbps
?
d) 350 Mbps
