# 2019A_FE_AM_78
When a company sells a product at 17 dollars per unit, and the fixed costs and the
variable costs are as shown in the tables below (in dollars), how many units does the
company have to sell to earn a profit of 42,000 dollars?
Fixed costs:
Administration costs 	130,000
R&D costs 	10,000
Variable costs per unit:
Raw material costs 	4
Administration costs 	3

a) 10,200
b) 14,200
c) 16,200
d) 18,200
?
d) 18,200
