# 2019A_FE_AM_11
Which of the following is the average cycles per instruction (CPI) of a computer that can
execute 1 billion instructions per second with a clock rate of 2.4 GHz?

a) 0.04
b) 0.12
c) 2.4
d) 25
?
c) 2.4
