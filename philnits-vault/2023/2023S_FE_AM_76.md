# 2023S_FE_AM_76
To secure transparency in business administration, which of the following is a concept
that supervises and monitors a company’s activities from the perspective of questions such as
for whom the company runs its business, how top management is structured, and whether the
organization has self-improvement capabilities?

a) Core competence
b) Corporate governance
c) Corporate identity
d) Stakeholder analysis
?
b) Corporate governance
