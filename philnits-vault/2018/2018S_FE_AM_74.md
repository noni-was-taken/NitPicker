# 2018S_FE_AM_74
Which of the following is the appropriate role of a facilitator in a meeting?

a) To act as the chairperson and control the discussions so as to work out a conclusion that matches the intentions of the management
b) To encourage the participants of the meeting to put forth their remarks and organize the flow of discussions from a neutral and impartial position
c) To give advice only in discussions related to specific areas in which the facilitator specializes, such as technical or legal fields
d) To offer specialized support for the administration of a meeting, such as adjusting the schedule, preparing materials, or creating minutes.
?
b) To encourage the participants of the meeting to put forth their remarks and organize the flow of discussions from a neutral and impartial position
