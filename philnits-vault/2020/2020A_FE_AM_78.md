# 2020A_FE_AM_78
Which of the following is a method of inventory asset valuation to calculate the inventory
unit price by the expression as shown below each time that a new inventory asset is received?
(The costs of goods received + The costs of goods in stock) 	/
(The quantity of goods received + The quantity of goods in stock)

a) Final acquisition cost method (Final purchase cost method)
b) First-in first-out method
c) Moving average method
d) Periodic average method
?
c) Moving average method
