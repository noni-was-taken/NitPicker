cost per unit of $48, and a selling price per unit of $80. Which of the following is the point
of sales ($) to become break-even?

a) 240,000 [2016S_79_opt_a.png]
b) 320,000 [2016S_79_opt_b.png]
c) 480,000 [2016S_79_opt_c.png]
d) 560,000 [2016S_79_opt_d.png]
