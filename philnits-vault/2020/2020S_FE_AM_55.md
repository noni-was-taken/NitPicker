# 2020S_FE_AM_55
In ITIL, the reduction in the index value expressing maintainability is cited as an example
of a key performance index (KPI) in availability management. Which of the following
corresponds to such an index?

a) Mean time between failures
b) Mean time between service incidents
c) Mean time to restore service
d) Number of interruptions in a certain period of time
?
c) Mean time to restore service
