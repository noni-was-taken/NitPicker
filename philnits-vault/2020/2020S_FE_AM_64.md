# 2020S_FE_AM_64
Which of the following is an explanation of BYOD (bring your own device)?

a) Carrying information devices loaned by the company for use in work
b) Configuring a usage environment for the personal use of personally-owned information devices
c) Using employees’ personally-owned information devices for work
d) Using laptop computers or other devices owned by the company for personal use outside of the company
?
c) Using employees’ personally-owned information devices for work
