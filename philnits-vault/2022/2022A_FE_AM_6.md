# 2022A_FE_AM_6
There are three (3) blue balls and four (4) green balls in a box. When two balls are randomly
taken out from the box, what is the probability that both are blue?

a) 2/49
b) 1/15
c) 1/7
d) 9/49
?
c) 1/7
