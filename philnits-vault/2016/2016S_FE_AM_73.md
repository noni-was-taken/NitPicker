# 2016S_FE_AM_73
Which of the following is an explanation of an e-marketplace?

a) It is a method in which many sellers and buyers meet in a market set up on the Internet and trade directly without an intermediate distributor. [2016S_73_opt_a.png]
b) It is a method that aims for overall optimization from material procurement to product distribution by sharing information such as ordering, inventory, sales, and logistics between a company and its business partners. [2016S_73_opt_b.png]
c) It is a method that converts information concerning business transactions into a standardized format and electronically exchanges data such as estimation, ordering, shipment and delivery, and payment between companies. [2016S_73_opt_c.png]
d) It is a method that runs a sales promotion campaign on the Internet and then draws customers to a real store to encourage them to make purchases. [2016S_73_opt_d.png]
?
a) It is a method in which many sellers and buyers meet in a market set up on the Internet and trade directly without an intermediate distributor.
